# Literature abstract recovery and re-grade (EXP-0063 + EXP-0064), 2026-09-20

Generated by `02_shared_code/registry/record_exp0063_0064_literature.py` from
`00_program_registry/EXP0063_s2_summary.json` and `00_program_registry/EXP0064_regrade_delta.json`.

## Why

EXP-0056 could not retrieve an abstract for **94 of 287**
retrieved records, and EXP-0057's grading rule cannot honestly read an overlap level from a title alone: those records
were marked `graded_on=title_only` and left undecidable. That is a hole in the literature pass of all five papers, so
the premise "no other abstract source is reachable" was measured instead of assumed:
`api.openalex.org` does not connect from this host, while `api.semanticscholar.org` answers and its batch endpoint
returns stored abstract text. No inference API is involved - this is a bibliographic metadata service.

## What was done

* `02_shared_code/literature/fetch_abstracts_s2.py` (EXP-0063): DOI/arXiv batch lookup in groups of 50, three tries per
  batch, three-way per-batch status (`informative` / `all_null` / `transport_error`), checkpointed per batch. The
  frozen `literature_abstracts.csv` is never modified; results land in `literature_abstracts_s2.csv`.
* `02_shared_code/literature/regrade_with_recovered_abstracts.py` (EXP-0064): merges the recovered text into
  `literature_abstracts_v2.csv` and re-runs **the imported, unmodified EXP-0057 rule** (not a copy) to
  `literature_graded_v2.csv`, then aligns the two graded tables by position with the alignment asserted.

## Hand verification of the records that became L3

- **P004 `Amin2026m`** -> `level_final=L2` (rule said L3). controlled 2,400-rating multi-language study of rubric-guided AI-assisted feedback to raise the reproducibility of human code-quality ratings. This paper's claim is limited to: reproducibility of in-study human ratings under two conditions; P004's population is third-party published scores with no condition contrast, and it adds the criterion-validity test they do not run.
- **P004 `Enguehard2025l`** -> `level_final=L2` (rule said L3). reference-free legal-domain judge (Legal Data Points) built because judge reliability differs in legal text. This paper's claim is limited to: domain-specific judge construction; P004 does not propose a judge and its object is the shipped score itself.
- **P004 `Gu2026a`** -> `level_final=L2` (rule said L3). survey that defines and classifies LLM-as-a-judge systems and proposes a reliability-oriented benchmark. This paper's claim is limited to: a forward-looking design framework; P004 is an audit of the measurement properties of scores that already ship, and cites this survey as the framing it argues against rather than builds on.
- **P004 `Haldar2025r`** -> `level_final=L3` (rule said L3). quantifies low intra-rater reliability of LLM judges across repeated runs on standard NLG benchmarks and asks whether judicious use is still possible. This paper's claim is limited to: studies scores the authors can re-run; P004 studies scores already shipped in published artefacts, where no re-run of the generating configuration is available, and tests criterion validity (does the score track anything), not only self-consistency.
- **P005 `Duan2024m`** -> `level_final=L3` (rule said L3). ModelGo audits licence conflicts and legal risk across the software, database and model components of an ML project, and derives guidelines from five real-world use cases. This paper's claim is limited to: consumes licence declarations as given and reasons over conflicts; P005 measures the declarations themselves - whether rights/provenance metadata is present, attributable to an artefact, and verifiable - and reports the cases where it is not, which is the precondition ModelGo assumes.

## Two bugs this pass caught about itself

1. `paper_id` in the literature tables names the **paper** (five distinct values across 287
   records) and no column is unique, so the first run of EXP-0063 keyed its per-record state on `paper_id` and
   silently collapsed 89 lookup results into 3 rows. Fixed by addressing records as `{index}|{cite_key}`; the
   collapsed output is archived at `results_raw/superseded/EXP0063_literature_abstracts_s2_run1_collapsed_by_paper_id.csv`.
2. The first version of the EXP-0064 delta made the same mistake on the graded tables and reported 5 records. The
   re-run asserts positional alignment on `(paper_id, cite_key, title)` for all 287 rows before
   comparing anything.

## What this does not claim

It does not mean the literature pass is now complete: 64 records are still
title-only, the recovered set covers only what one secondary index holds, and a rule grade is a screen, not a verdict -
`level_final` is authoritative and is filled only for the records read above.
